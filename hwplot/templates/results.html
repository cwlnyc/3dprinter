Printing

  1. Put the G-code on the printer's SD card (or send it with OctoPrint / Mainsail).
    2. {% if placement|length > 2 and placement[2] == 'flipped' %}
  2. Turn the sheet round: face up, with its title (top edge) towards the front of the printer, so the page's top-left corner is at the front right. The pen only reaches the right part of the bed, and this way round the blank side of the page lies on the bed instead of hanging off the side into the frame.
    3. {% else %}
  3. Lay the homework face up, top edge towards the back of the printer.
    4. {% endif %}
  4. {{ where }} Roughly is enough: the printer's stops show you exactly where. The page hangs off the front and back of the bed, which is fine. (In printer coordinates: X {{ placement[0] }}, Y {{ placement[1] }}.)
  5. Tape it down along every edge that's on the bed (painter's tape), and where it crosses the bed's edge, so nothing can lift. Before starting, slide the bed front to back by hand and check the overhang doesn't catch on anything.
  6. Start the print. After homing it makes two stops, with the pen hovering just above the paper. The screen says what to line up:
    • "Top-left corner here" (or "Pen at blank …" when the corner is out of reach): slide the paper until that spot is right under the pen tip, then press the knob.
    • "Left edge under pen" or "Turn: blank …": without moving the first spot, turn the paper slightly until that edge or answer line is under the pen tip. (It means the page's own left edge, which is on your right when the sheet is turned round.) That squares it up. Tape it down, then press the knob.
  7. Stay nearby for the first few letters.
