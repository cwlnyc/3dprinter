Printing

  1. Put the G-code on the printer's SD card (or send it with OctoPrint / Mainsail).
  2. Lay the homework face up, top edge towards the back of the printer. Its top-left corner goes at bed X {{ placement[0] }}, Y {{ placement[1] }}. A Letter page is bigger than most beds, so it will hang over the edge. That's fine: the answers are centred on the bed. {% if placement[0]|float < 0 %}
    Negative X means the corner sits {{ '%.1f'|format(-(placement[0]|float)) }} mm left of the bed's left edge, past where the pen can reach. Measure it with a ruler from the edge of the bed.{% endif %}
  3. Tape the corners down so it can't slide.
  4. Start the print. It homes, moves the pen over the paper's top-left corner (if that's on the bed) and pauses. Slide the paper until the corner is right under the pen tip, then resume.
  5. Stay nearby for the first few letters.
